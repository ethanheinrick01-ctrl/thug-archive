# Song context

Research coverage is deliberately partial: 15 archive entries currently have a song-level community-era attribution. Missing dates stay unknown. The community tracker is not an artist/label session log. Its file dates, preview years, and era groupings are distinct from an official release date or a proven recording date. A matching song name does not authenticate the exact archive mix.

Sources: the public Young Thug community tracker linked from https://www.unreleased.world/youngthug and its era pages. Retrieved November 1, 2026. Original research excerpts are retained locally outside the repository.

The guitar-led collection contains Birthday Boy, Rosetta Stone, and To Me, based on listening to archive excerpts, not an inferred tempo or album name. It is a starter collection, not an exhaustive instrument analysis. To Me is stored as a SabSad edit, retained as such.

Birthday Boy is attributed to UY SCUTI by the tracker. It is not labeled a Beautiful Thugger Girls outtake. Love You More has several distinct tracker entries: the four-minute archive song has not been assigned the separate Punk recording's history.

Artist browsing uses existing archive credits plus the researched entries. Missing and incomplete credits remain visible as unconfirmed instead of assigning all songs to Young Thug. Known details are shared across rows, likes, playlists, queue, menus, and player views.
