# Thug Archive UI profile

Source: accepted discovery plan and docs/DESIGN.md.

- Pattern: List / Index for discovery; native dialogs for filters and song details.
- Preserve charcoal/olive/lime archive identity, Preact, Lucide, original artwork and persistent audio.
- Canvas #111210; list surface #191b18; text #f4f3ec; supporting text #a6aca1; selected/play/focus #c7f36a.
- UI body 16px, secondary 14px, metadata minimum 12px. Weights 400/500/600; existing display headings may use 700.
- Hit areas at least 44px. Visible focus, keyboard operation, dialog focus return, reduced motion, and safe-area clearance. This is an acceptance target, not certification.
- Straightforward music-library copy. No decorative badge clouds, gradients, fake statistics, invented moods/credits, or new icon packages.
- Recordings stay distinct. Historical evidence, listening judgments and audio estimates stay separate. No invented certainty or historical timestamps.
- Test at 375px, 638px, 1280px and short phone height. Physical iPhone checks remain distinct from viewport testing.
- All review passes are manual in the current conversation. No reviewer subprocesses, subagents or paid model APIs.
